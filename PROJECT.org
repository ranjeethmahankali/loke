  on to the arcs and lines too, since the internal representation just
  interpolates between 0 and 1, and we just pretend from the outside that they
  are length parametrized. It feels better to just parametrize Arc and
  LineSegment from 0 to 1, and just let the Spline do its thing.

  This basically involves applying a transformation matrix to all the vectors in the curve. Need to

  I could implement this as a general library feature that takes a matrix and modifies the
  curve. Unsure. Requires more thought. I need a way to judge the incoming matrix whether it soes a
